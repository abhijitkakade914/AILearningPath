# Comprehensive Guide to Becoming a General AI Architect

## Foundational Knowledge (6-12 months)

### 1. Core AI/ML Concepts
- [Machine Learning Algorithms](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/user_guide.html) (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement)
- [Deep Learning Architectures](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/deep_learning_60min_blitz.html) (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers)
- [Generative AI Fundamentals](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/gan) (GANs, VAEs, Diffusion Models)
- [Mathematics for AI](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/mathematics-for-machine-learning) (linear algebra, calculus, probability)

### 2. Programming & Tools
- [Python for AI](https://www.learnpython.org/) (NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn)
- AI Frameworks: [TensorFlow](https://www.tensorflow.org/learn), [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/learn), [Keras](https://keras.io/getting_started/)
- MLOps Tools: [MLflow](https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/tutorials-and-examples/tutorial.html), [Kubeflow](https://www.kubeflow.org/docs/started/), [Airflow](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/tutorial.html)

## Intermediate Specializations (6-12 months)

### 3. Cloud-Agnostic AI Architecture
- [Multi-cloud AI Deployment Patterns](https://cloud.google.com/architecture/ai/multi-cloud-ai-architecture)
- Containerization: [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/get-started/) and [Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/docs/tutorials/)
- [AI Workload Optimization](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/gpu-accelerated-applications/ai/)

### 4. Security-Focused AI
- [Adversarial Machine Learning](https://cleverhans.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
- [Model Poisoning Prevention](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.00308)
- Privacy Techniques: [Federated Learning](https://www.tensorflow.org/federated), [Differential Privacy](https://github.com/google/differential-privacy)
- [Secure Model Deployment](https://owasp.org/www-project-machine-learning-security-top-10/)

## Advanced Architectures (6-12 months)

### 5. Enterprise AI Systems
- [Scalable AI Pipeline Design](https://www.kubeflow.org/docs/components/pipelines/)
- [Feature Stores](https://www.featurestore.org/) and [Model Registries](https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/model-registry.html)
- [AI Governance Frameworks](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)

### 6. Generative AI Architectures
- [LLM Orchestration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401) (RAG, fine-tuning)
- [Multi-Agent Systems](https://www.multiagent.com/)
- [Ethical AI Design](https://www.partnershiponai.org/resources/)
